Privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Scrypted Nest is a private, single-household integration run by the owner of keysenye.com. It uses the Google Smart Device Management API to access the owner's own Google Nest devices.

What data is accessed

Only Nest devices the account owner explicitly shares through Google's Partner Connections: camera live streams, snapshots, and motion, person, sound and doorbell events, plus home and room names.

How it is used

The data is used only to show those cameras and events in the household's Apple Home app, through Scrypted software running on the household's own computer. Recordings, if enabled, are handled by Apple's HomeKit Secure Video in the owner's iCloud account.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or accessed by anyone outside the household.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections or in your Google Account's third-party access settings.
